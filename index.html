Auto Shorts

Personal video publishing tool · Google Cloud project “VOD-Uploader”

Auto Shorts is a personal tool that automatically uploads its owner's own short videos and stream recordings to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, on a schedule defined in advance.

It runs only on the owner's computer. It is not offered to the public and does not collect any data about other people.

YouTube — This tool uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
